## Back to the basics...

Manufacturing chips sounds exactly as hard as it actually is and it's really important to understand how we make them before we design them so we can understand their nuances, limitations and a better understanding of the underlying fundamentals. 

I would highly suggest watching [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aBDJQ9NYTEU) by Matt Venn, a notable chip designer on how chips are manufactured, it'll be the most useful 17 minutes of your life. If you're also a nerd like me, Veritasium has a [lovely video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MiUHjLxm3V0) on ASML's most complicated machines that are used to manufacture the most complicated chips on the planet. The [resource section](/resources) also has a lot of really useful video's, and miscellaneous sites if you'd like to learn more about the material.
