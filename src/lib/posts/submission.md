## Submit your design!

Any teenager from the ages of 13 - 18 is allowed to submit an ASIC design to get on our shared chip or receive an FPGA board!

Submitting is quite simple:
1. Create a GitHub repository with your chip design, I would suggest referencing any of the past chip GitHub repositories on the Tiny Tapeout website: https://tinytapeout.com/chips/. Your repository should have a complete design which means it's been simulated and appears to work in GTKWave, and passes all of the GitHub workflows, which should leave you with a physical preview of the logic gates for your chip, feel free to take a look at my first ASIC design: https://github.com/KaiPereira/SPI-Slave-ASIC.
2. Create a write-up of your project and make it publicly available somewhere the internet that's not GitHub, even share it around if you like. Learn how to create your write-up [here](/write-up).
3. Submit to the form for an FPGA board [here](form_for_fpga_board) if it's your first design, or the form for the shared chip [here](form_for_shared_chip) if it's your second design and you've gotten both your first and second design successfully working on an FPGA board.
